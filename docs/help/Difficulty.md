# Difficulty

Analyses how hard the focused lane is, in a window of its own that is wide enough to see the graph over the song in detail.

**Open it from:** Chart Builder, **View**, then **Difficulty window**, or the open-in-window button in the Difficulty tab. It stays open and follows the editor when another song is opened.

## What's in it

The same as the Difficulty tab in the editor's right panel:

- a rank from 0 to 6 (7 or more is an overchart)
- a spider graph of attributes, for the whole chart, the selection, or the playhead
- a graph of difficulty over the song

Space plays and pauses.
