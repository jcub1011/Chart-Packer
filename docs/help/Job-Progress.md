# Job Progress

Lists the results of the current or last pack or unpack job, with problems first.

**Open it from:** click the status bar at the bottom of the Chart Packer window.

## What's in it

- A search box and chips to show Packed (or Unpacked), Skipped, or Failed or Canceled songs.
- A row per song with its status, name, details, and any warnings or errors.
- Double-click a row to select that song in the library.
- **Cancel** (while a job runs) and **Close**.

Closing the window leaves a running job running.
