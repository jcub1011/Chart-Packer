# Keyboard Shortcuts

Lists every shortcut in the app, and lets you change them. Both apps share this window, and shortcuts they have in common, such as Save, are shared.

**Open it from:** the **Edit** menu, then **Shortcuts…**.

## What's in it

- A category list and a search box.
- For each shortcut: change it, remove it, or add an alternate. **Restore Defaults** resets one.
- **Import** and **Export** (JSON files), **Reset All**, **OK** and **Cancel**.

Some shortcuts, such as Undo and Redo, are fixed. A shortcut that is already used asks whether to move it or keep both. Keys the system reserves are refused.

Press **F1** in any window to open its help page.
